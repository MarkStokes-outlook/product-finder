# Black Friday MVP

Read-only seasonal view over existing active wanted catalogue tracking rows. Unresolved active watches are shown separately as product-identification work, not deals. No new source credentials, schema, marketplace scoring changes or affiliate ranking changes. Uses the existing retailer discovery / approval / refresh pipeline. Black Friday is the Friday after the fourth Thursday in November (for 2026, 27 November); the display window begins seven days earlier and ends on Cyber Monday. The historical comparator is fixed before the event and is never based on RRP.

Freshness, evidence thresholds and reduction bands are initial conservative product choices, not empirically calibrated deal scores. Historical records have domain rather than URL provenance, and candidate approval can retain an earlier price on failed refresh; the UI explicitly calls these recorded observations, not verified historical offers. Current successful price checks are necessary but not proof of availability or delivered cost.

Next: exact URL/variant/stock/delivery provenance, multiple approved retailers per product, season-specific discovery, bounded refresh jobs, persistent deduplicated alerts, and historical comparison against independently sourced data. Do not send purchase alerts on the basis of a price-only observation.
